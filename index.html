⚖️ Estimand Intelligence — Why the Same Endpoint Can Mean Different Things
ICH E9(R1) · Critical for interpreting IBD remission rates across TL1A trials
Essential BD Context ▼
An estimand defines the exact clinical question a trial is trying to answer — not just the endpoint, but how the trial handles real-world disruptions like rescue medication use, dose changes, discontinuations, and dropouts. Two IBD trials can both report "Week 52 remission" while answering fundamentally different questions. ICH E9(R1) (2019) requires sponsors to pre-specify the estimand before the trial starts.
Composite
Most common in IBD. Intercurrent events (rescue steroids, prohibited meds, discontinuation) are incorporated into the outcome — patients who need rescue are counted as non-responders, regardless of their actual Week 52 status. Measures real-world treatment success including tolerability. Industry standard for UC/CD Phase 2b/3.
Treatment Policy
ITT regardless of intercurrent events. All patients analyzed based on original assignment, whatever happened — rescue meds, dose changes, discontinuation. Measures the drug's effect in practice, not under ideal conditions. More common in respiratory than IBD. Can appear to inflate remission rates if patients received rescue.
Hypothetical
Ideal efficacy if the intercurrent event hadn't occurred. Estimates what remission would look like if no patient needed rescue or switched therapy. Useful for mechanism understanding and dose-finding. Rarely used as primary estimand in late-stage IBD; can produce higher, less clinically meaningful efficacy numbers.
While on Treatment
Outcome measured only while patient stays on therapy. Excludes post-discontinuation outcomes. Underestimates real-world effectiveness for chronic diseases with high discontinuation; rarely used as primary in IBD.
⚠️ BD read-through for IBD: When comparing TL1A remission rates across tulisokibart (Merck), duvakitug (Teva/Sanofi), and afimkibart (Roche), verify that all three are using a composite estimand with the same intercurrent event definition. A composite-based 35% remission rate is not comparable to a treatment-policy-based 45% remission rate — the second number is answering a different, easier question. This is critical when using monotherapy context data to inform bispecific design assumptions. Future TL1A × IL-23p19 Phase 2 trials should pre-specify composite estimands to align with the emerging IBD standard.

⚖️ Estimand Intelligence — Why Exacerbation Rates Are Not Always Comparable
ICH E9(R1) · Critical context for interpreting TSLP and IL-33 respiratory trial data
Essential BD Context ▼
Respiratory trials for TSLP and IL-33 inhibitors predominantly use a treatment policy estimand — they count exacerbations regardless of whether patients changed ICS doses, used rescue OCS, or discontinued the study drug. This is why tezepelumab (NAVIGATOR) could demonstrate efficacy across all asthma endotypes even in patients who adjusted background therapy. However, different trials handle OCS reduction, COPD exacerbation definitions, and hospitalization severity thresholds differently — making cross-trial comparisons treacherous without understanding the exact estimand.
Treatment Policy
Standard for exacerbation endpoints in asthma/COPD. Counts all exacerbations regardless of background therapy changes or rescue med use. Pragmatic — reflects real-world use. Most approved TSLP/IL-33 assets use this for primary endpoint. Tezepelumab (NAVIGATOR), itepekimab (QUEST), tozorakimab (RESCUE-COPD), astegolimab all use treatment policy as primary.
Composite
Used for OCS reduction and steroid-sparing endpoints. If a patient escalates OCS (intercurrent event), it's incorporated into the composite outcome as treatment failure. Common for atopic dermatitis and severe asthma OCS tapering trials. TSLP×IL-33 bispecifics targeting COPD may encounter composite estimands when exacerbation-triggered hospitalization is incorporated into the primary outcome.
Hypothetical
What would happen if background therapy hadn't changed? Rarely used as primary in respiratory. Sometimes used as secondary to understand the drug's mechanism independent of ICS/OCS adjustments. Useful for Phase 2 dose-finding in COPD. Can produce optimistic efficacy estimates not replicable in broader populations.
While on Treatment
Annualized exacerbation rate while receiving study drug. Used in trials with planned treatment cessation or long wash-out periods. Underestimates benefit if exacerbation risk rebounds post-discontinuation — relevant for long-acting TSLP inhibitors like QX031N, whose extended PK profile may require specific estimand consideration.
⚠️ BD read-through for Respiratory: Tezepelumab's landmark "works across all endotypes" story is only valid under its treatment policy estimand — patients whose ICS doses changed mid-trial were still counted, making the result more pragmatic. If a TSLP×IL-33 bispecific uses a hypothetical estimand that excludes patients who escalated ICS, it may report better-looking efficacy numbers that aren't comparable to NAVIGATOR data. Additionally, COPD exacerbation definitions vary significantly (Type 1: antibiotics only; Type 2: OCS; Type 3: hospitalization) — trials using different severity thresholds are not cross-comparable even if both use treatment policy estimands. Chinese companies designing TSLP bispecific Phase 2 protocols for COPD should align their exacerbation definition and estimand with the tezepelumab NAVIGATOR standard to maximize regulatory and partner credibility.

BD Intelligence · Key Takeaways
May 2026
Actionable intelligence for Chinese BD professionals
  • Qyuns has built the definitive Chinese TSLP playbook — and it's transferable. Three deals across TSLP×IL-33 (Roche), TSLP×IL-13 (Windward), and TL1A×IL-23p19 (Caldera), all retaining China rights. For Chinese biotech clients asking how to approach global partnership strategy, Qyuns is the reference case: build differentiated bispecifics, license global rights early at preclinical stage, retain China, use milestone payments to fund domestic development.
  • Roche and Windward Bio are the most active TSLP buyers — and their pipelines are now filling up. Roche has astegolimab (anti-ST2) + QX031N (TSLP×IL-33). Windward has HBM9378 (anti-TSLP mono) + QX027N (TSLP×IL-13). This means other TSLP bispecific assets may need to find different strategic homes — Pfizer, Lilly, AstraZeneca (which already has tezepelumab), and Sanofi are the most likely next acquirers.
  • COPD is the deal-driver, not asthma. Asthma is well-served by tezepelumab and dupilumab. COPD remains a massive unmet need with no approved biologic targeting TSLP specifically — Qyuns/Roche and Harbour+Kelun/Windward are both explicitly targeting COPD. When pitching TSLP-related assets to clients, the COPD angle drives deal rationale and justifies Phase 3 investment scale.
  • Tezepelumab's "all-endotype" story is estimand-dependent — and it's a BD talking point. NAVIGATOR used a treatment policy estimand that counted exacerbations even when patients changed background ICS therapy. This pragmatic design is the reason the "works regardless of baseline eosinophils" label held up post-approval. Chinese companies designing TSLP bispecific trials should replicate this estimand choice if they want a similarly broad label. A hypothetical or while-on-treatment estimand might inflate Phase 2 numbers but would undermine the path to a broad approval claim — and sophisticated partners will notice the difference.
