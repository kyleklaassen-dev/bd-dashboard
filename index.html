Composite
Most common in IBD. Treatment Policy
ITT regardless of intercurrent events. All patients analyzed based on original assignment, whatever happened â rescue meds, dose changes, discontinuation. Measures the drug's effect in practice, not under ideal conditions. More common in respiratory (exacerbation trials) than IBD. Can inflate apparent remission rates if patients got rescue.
Hypothetical

Useful for mechanism of action understanding and dose-finding. Rarely used as primary estimand in late-stage IBD; can produce higher, less clinically meaningful efficacy numbers.
While on Treatment
Outcome measured only while patient stays on therapy. Excludes post-discontinuation outcomes. Relevant for understanding pharmacological effect without confounding from treatment switch. Underestimates real-world effectiveness for chronic diseases with high discontinuation; rarely used as primary in IBD.
