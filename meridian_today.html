A molecule called IMVT-1402 just produced something the rheumatology field has not seen from an FcRn inhibitor: ACR50 responses in more than half of patients who had already failed both anti-TNF and JAK inhibitor therapy. Immunovant disclosed the Phase 2b data on Wednesday, and the stock moved 32% in a single session. Two weeks ago, the same company was paying $39 million to bury batoclimab after dual Phase 3 failures in thyroid eye disease. The pivot is complete, and it is working.

That contrast — the same target, different molecule, different indication, opposite outcome — is the cleanest illustration this week of a principle Kyle encountered in MF720: strategic pivot is not retreat. It is resource reallocation toward higher-probability value creation. Meanwhile, the IL-4Rα space is getting crowded in exactly the right way, with rademikibart posting Phase 3 numbers that make dupilumab look like a first draft, and Pfizer’s trispecific tilrekimig heading to Phase 3 with a mechanism that bridges TSLP and IL-4Rα in a single molecule. The tea is hot. The week earned its Friday.

FcRn: IMVT-1402 Validates the Pivot in Difficult-to-Treat RA

FOCUS AREA: FcRn · AUTOIMMUNE IgG DISEASES

Immunovant disclosed preliminary Week 16 results from the open-label Phase 2b study of IMVT-1402 in difficult-to-treat rheumatoid arthritis on May 20, and the data landed hard. Among 165 evaluable patients — all of whom had failed at least two prior advanced therapies including biologic and targeted synthetic DMARDs — ACR20, ACR50, and ACR70 response rates were 72.7%, 54.5%, and 35.8%, respectively. The consistency across subgroups is notable: patients who had failed both JAK inhibitors and anti-TNF agents showed response rates comparable to the overall population, suggesting that IgG clearance via FcRn blockade addresses a disease axis that prior mechanism classes leave untouched. (BioWorld)

IMVT-1402 is a subcutaneous anti-FcRn antibody with what Immunovant describes as an albumin-sparing profile — meaning it preferentially accelerates IgG degradation without proportionally depleting serum albumin, a limitation that constrained batoclimab’s therapeutic index. The pharmacological differentiation matters because chronic dosing in RA requires sustained IgG reduction over months to years without the hypoalbuminemia-driven adverse events that can accumulate. Immunovant will provide further clinical updates in H2 2026.

The strategic context: argenx’s efgartigimod (VYVGART) is approved in myasthenia gravis, CIDP, and ITP. UCB’s rozanolixizumab is approved in MG. Johnson & Johnson’s nipocalimab is under review for hemolytic disease of the fetus and newborn. But no FcRn inhibitor has yet established efficacy in rheumatoid arthritis, particularly in the difficult-to-treat population. If these data hold in a controlled setting, IMVT-1402 would open an entirely new indication class for the FcRn mechanism.

BD signal: The RA data reposition IMVT-1402 as a potential first-in-class FcRn inhibitor for rheumatology. For Ailux’s FcRn program, this validates the mechanism in a large-market autoimmune indication beyond neurology and raises the bar for any new FcRn entrant to demonstrate differentiation on albumin sparing and indication breadth. The stock’s 32% move reflects the market recognizing that FcRn is no longer a neurology-only mechanism.

IL-4Rα: Rademikibart Posts Best-in-Class Phase 3 Numbers; Pfizer’s Trispecific Follows

FOCUS AREA: IL-4Rα · ATOPY (AD, PRURIGO, ALLERGIC ASTHMA)

Connect Biopharma and its partner Simcere Pharmaceutical presented the 52-week results from RADIANT-AD, the Phase 3 study of rademikibart monotherapy in moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis, at the AAD Annual Meeting on March 28. The headline number: 96.6% of patients achieved EASI-75 at week 52. For context, dupilumab’s LIBERTY AD CHRONOS study reported EASI-75 rates of approximately 65% at week 52 with concomitant topical corticosteroids. Rademikibart achieved this as monotherapy. (GlobeNewsWire)

The mechanistic basis is structural. Rademikibart is a fully human monoclonal antibody targeting IL-4Rα with approximately twice the binding affinity of dupilumab, achieved through an optimized epitope. Higher-affinity binding translates to more complete blockade of both IL-4 and IL-13 signaling, driving deeper and more durable skin clearance. Safety was comparable to placebo through 52 weeks.

The second development: Pfizer’s tilrekimig, a first-in-class trispecific antibody simultaneously targeting IL-4, IL-13, and TSLP, reported positive Phase 2 results in moderate-to-severe AD in March 2026 and is advancing into a pivotal Phase 3 study later this year. (Pfizer)

TSLP: PASSAGE Validates the Mechanism Broadly; Windward and Uniquity Close In

FOCUS AREA: TSLP · RESPIRATORY (ASTHMA, COPD)

The PASSAGE Phase 4 study of tezepelumab (TEZSPIRE, Amgen/AstraZeneca) was published in the American Journal of Respiratory and Critical Care Medicine on May 18 and presented at ATS 2026 this week. The study enrolled a diverse real-world severe asthma population including Black/African American patients, adolescents, current smokers, and asthma-COPD overlap patients. 52-week results: >50% reduction in systemic corticosteroid exposure, 74% achieving clinically meaningful ACQ-6 improvement, 80% reporting quality-of-life gains. (Healio)

PASSAGE validates TSLP inhibition as a broad-spectrum mechanism across inflammatory phenotypes. Windward Bio secured $165M in crossover financing (May 4) to advance WIN378, a long-acting anti-TSLP antibody for twice-yearly SC dosing, into Phase 3 asthma (Q4 2026) and Phase 2 SIRIUS in COPD (before June 2026). (Fierce Biotech)

Uniquity Bio (Blackstone Life Sciences) is approaching a key readout: Phase 2 RAINIER data for solrikitug in asthma (H1 2026), with COPD data (ZION) following in H2. Solrikitug demonstrated 15-fold greater potency than tezepelumab in inhibiting TSLPR/IL-7R dimerization. (AJMC)

BD signal: PASSAGE’s phenotype-agnostic data expand the TSLP market. The competitive landscape is three-dimensional: frequency (WIN378, twice-yearly), potency (solrikitug, 15x tezepelumab), and breadth (tilrekimig, TSLP + IL-4/IL-13).

T-Cell Engineering: Cabaletta Unveils Conditioning-Free CAR-T Data Ahead of EULAR

FOCUS AREA: T-CELL ENGINEERING (ACE) · IMMUNE RESET (SLE, MYOSITIS)

Cabaletta Bio presented updated clinical and manufacturing data for rese-cel (resecabtagene autoleucel) at ASGCT 2026 on May 14. Preconditioning-free dosing data showed clinical responses without lymphodepleting chemotherapy, paralleling Fate Therapeutics’ FT819 data. The convergence suggests chemotherapy-free immune reset is becoming the new standard. (GlobeNewsWire)

EULAR London (June 3–6) is the next major catalyst. Cabaletta will present complete Phase 1/2 rese-cel data in lupus and systemic sclerosis and announce the second pivotal indication.

BD signal: Conditioning-free convergence de-risks the autoimmune cell therapy category. EULAR provides the first autologous vs. allogeneic comparison. The strategic question: dual-targeting (CD19 + CD38/BCMA) as the mechanistic requirement for durable reset.

CONCEPT TO HOLD

Strategic Pivot as Value Creation: The Immunovant Case Through MF720 Corporate Strategy

MF720 (Corporate Strategy) teaches that a strategic pivot is resource reallocation from lower to higher expected value. Immunovant’s trajectory is a textbook case: years invested in batoclimab for TED, mechanism proved insufficient, $39M exit payment, full consolidation around IMVT-1402. Ghemawat’s commitment under uncertainty: exiting was not costless, but continuing would have been costlier. The pivot to RA — ACR50 in 54.5% of multi-refractory patients, $700M+ market cap created in one session — validates the framework.

The lesson for Ailux: the quality of a pivot depends on the asset you pivot toward. IMVT-1402’s albumin-sparing profile is a pharmacological redesign, not a marketing claim. The pivot worked because the new molecule solved the predecessor’s problem.
