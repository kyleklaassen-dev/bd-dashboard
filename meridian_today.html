Large pharma's next-generation biologics strategies are diverging along a structural fault line this week — Novartis is narrowing its ex vivo CAR-T thesis to a single autoimmune disease bet while Regeneron is buying modality optionality at scale — and the common thread is that neither approach addresses the IBD combination-therapy question that will define asset valuation after the Merck TL1A readout in November. The week's signal cluster — Novartis's rap-cel pivot, PHE885's discontinuation, Regeneron's $2.3B Parabilis platform deal, and Immunovant's batoclimab wind-down — collectively maps a landscape in which large companies are reconfiguring their biologics architectures for 2028–2030 without placing chips on the single most valuable open question in GI: whether dual TL1A×IL-23p19 blockade outperforms sequential monotherapy in IBD.

That gap is Ailux's structural opening, but it is one that shrinks with every month of silence from Merck and Roche on their Phase 3 TL1A monotherapy programs. Neither company has updated enrollment, site count, or interim analysis timing this week — nor for the past several weeks — for ATLAS-UC or AMETRINE-2. In isolation, no-news-is-good-news applies: trials proceeding on schedule do not need public recalibration. But the compounding silence has a second-order implication. Neither Merck nor Roche is building a pre-readout BD narrative around TL1A. They are not seeding conference presentations, not disclosing biomarker strategies, not publicizing enrollment pace. This is the behavior of companies who have no reason to create optionality before their data speak — which means the monotherapy readouts will land as clean events without pre-positioned hedges. For a bispecific like SPY002, the cleanest monotherapy ceiling is actually the best outcome: it makes the combination delta measurable and the bispecific premium quantifiable.

Meanwhile, the week produced a cross-mechanism lesson in dosing design that sharpens SPY002's differentiation narrative. Immunovant's $39M write-off to terminate batoclimab was fundamentally a dosing-design failure — the step-down regimen from 680mg to 340mg allowed IgG rebound that erased the therapeutic effect in thyroid eye disease. On the opposite end, Upstream Bio's ATS 2026 VIBRANT sub-analysis confirmed that verekitug's Q12W extended-dosing regimen delivers durable TSLP blockade across comorbid asthma endpoints. The market is pricing dosing durability as a co-primary differentiator alongside target selection. For SPY002, whose SC bispecific format is designed to collapse the dosing burden of sequential IV/SC combination therapy, this week's evidence strengthens the case that convenience and dosing coherence are not secondary features — they are competitive moats.

The conspicuous absence from the week's signal map is AbbVie. Risankizumab remains the approved IL-23p19 standard of care in both UC and CD, yet AbbVie has generated no new signal — no combination trial disclosures, no expansion study updates, no conference abstracts — on extending risankizumab's IBD franchise. This is the posture of a company that believes its monospecific IL-23p19 franchise is secure through at least the Merck Phase 3 readout. If tulisokibart validates TL1A in November and the market immediately asks "what does TL1A plus IL-23 look like?", AbbVie will be the company with the most to lose (approved IL-23p19 monotherapy) and the most to gain (the obvious combination partner). AbbVie's silence now is pre-data positioning that could turn into aggressive BD motion by Q1 2027.

Mechanism Intelligence

CAR-T / Autoimmune: The T-Charge Thesis Narrows to a Single Bet

Novartis's autoimmune CAR-T strategy is now structurally fragile in a way it was not six months ago. The discontinuation of PHE885 — the BCMA-targeted T-Charge CAR-T for multiple myeloma — eliminates the only oncology program that gave the T-Charge platform diversification beyond autoimmune indications. Rap-cel (rapcabtagene autoleucel, CD19 autologous CAR-T) is now carrying the entirety of the T-Charge value proposition, with Phase 2 programs in lupus nephritis, SLE, systemic sclerosis, and myositis targeting a lupus nephritis BLA by 2028. The platform's core differentiation claim — that shortened ex vivo expansion preserves naïve and stem-cell memory T-cell phenotypes, producing more durable responses — is scientifically credible but has not yet been validated against the in vivo CAR-T approaches that Lilly, Gilead, AbbVie, BMS, and AstraZeneca have acquired in the last twelve months.

The critical observation is where this investment is going: SLE and systemic sclerosis, not IBD. CAR-T — whether ex vivo or in vivo — has no credible mechanistic path in inflammatory bowel disease. B-cell depletion via CD19-targeted CAR-T can reset the autoantibody-driven pathology of lupus or myositis, but IBD pathogenesis is driven by mucosal T-cell and innate immune dysregulation downstream of TL1A, IL-23, TNF, and integrin signaling. The autoimmune cell-therapy wave is absorbing multi-billion dollar investments that will raise the competitive ceiling for antibody-based approaches in SLE and related diseases — but it structurally cannot compete in the IBD space where SPY002 operates.

The remaining T-Charge pipeline — rap-cel plus the Legend Biotech DLL3 collaboration — leaves Novartis with minimal platform breadth. If rap-cel encounters manufacturing scalability issues (a persistent vulnerability of autologous CAR-T) or fails to demonstrate durability advantages over the in vivo approaches by 2027, Novartis will need external assets to sustain its autoimmune franchise. Their inflammation portfolio has a conspicuous IBD gap: ixekizumab targets IL-17A (a mechanism that worsened IBD outcomes in prior studies), and there is no disclosed IL-23 or TL1A program.

Novartis should be added to the prospective partner watch list, with engagement timing contingent on SPY002 Phase 2 data strength and rap-cel's 2027 trajectory. FcRn / IgG Depletion: Batoclimab's Failure Confirms the Dosing-Design Thesis

Immunovant's formal termination of batoclimab and the $39M wind-down payment to HanAll Biopharma close a chapter that was mechanistically foreseeable. Batoclimab's Phase 3 failure in thyroid eye disease was not a failure of FcRn inhibition as a mechanism — it was a failure of the step-down dosing regimen, which allowed pathogenic IgG to rebound as FcRn recycling restored IgG half-life after dose reduction. Immunovant's pivot to IMVT-1402, using a 600mg continuous-dosing regimen that eliminates the step-down, is an implicit acknowledgment that the mechanism works but the pharmacokinetic design did not. The Graves' disease pivotal program with 2027 topline readout will test whether continuous dosing resolves the problem — or whether batoclimab's liabilities extend beyond regimen design to molecule-specific properties.

Read alongside Upstream Bio's Q12W verekitug data at ATS — where extended dosing intervals delivered durable anti-TSLP activity without efficacy erosion — a cross-mechanism principle emerges. The biologics market in 2026 is punishing suboptimal dosing design with program-killing severity. Batoclimab's failure and verekitug's success bracket the spectrum: the mechanism that gets the dosing wrong loses $39M and a pipeline position; the mechanism that gets it right enters Phase 3 with a clean BD narrative. For any bispecific antibody entering clinical development, dosing coherence is now a gating criterion, not an optimization exercise.

BD LENS

SPY002's SC bispecific format — designed to deliver simultaneous TL1A and IL-23p19 blockade in a single injection — gains differentiation value from batoclimab's failure. The alternative to a bispecific in IBD is sequential combination therapy: one IV or SC monoclonal for TL1A, another for IL-23p19, on separate dosing schedules with separate PK profiles. The market is now demonstrably intolerant of dosing complexity that creates pharmacokinetic gaps. SPY002's Phase 2 data package must therefore demonstrate not only combination efficacy superiority but that a single dosing regimen maintains adequate trough levels for both targets — dosing durability data will be as important as endoscopic improvement rates for BD discussions.

TSLP / Respiratory: Verekitug Phase 3 Confirmation Anchors the Valuation Benchmark

The ATS 2026 cycle that this week's earlier issues tracked is now complete with a coda that matters for BD pricing across biologics. Upstream Bio's confirmation that Phase 3 registrational trials for verekitug will initiate in Q1 2027 in both severe asthma and CRSwNP — paired with the VIBRANT sub-analysis showing 83% reduction in rescue corticosteroid/surgery need (nominal p=0.027) among CRSwNP patients with comorbid asthma — establishes a live reference point for what a Phase 2-validated biologic with extended-dosing differentiation is worth at the pivotal-entry inflection. Upstream Bio's current valuation trajectory, as a single-asset company entering dual-indication Phase 3, will anchor BD conversations for any other Phase 2 biologic approaching the same transition — including SPY002.

The Q1 2027 Phase 3 start also locks in H2 2026 as the partnership window that Thursday's issue flagged. AstraZeneca (tezepelumab franchise owner), GSK (depemokimab in development), and Sanofi (dupilumab franchise) are the likely suitors, and each has different strategic urgency. AstraZeneca would be buying verekitug to protect its TSLP franchise from a Q12W dosing-superiority threat; GSK would be buying market access in eosinophilic respiratory disease; Sanofi would be diversifying beyond IL-4Rα. The structure and premium of any H2 deal will calibrate how the market values Phase 2-to-Phase 3 transitions for extended-dosing biologics — a direct read-through for SPY002's own eventual BD pricing.

BD LENS

The verekitug Phase 3 confirmation creates a quantifiable valuation benchmark that Ailux should explicitly incorporate into BD modeling. A single-asset company entering dual-indication Phase 3 with clean Phase 2 dose-response and differentiated dosing — which is the profile SPY002 would present after a positive Phase 2 — will be priced against Upstream's deal or market cap at the time of its partnership. If Upstream secures a deal in H2 2026 before SPY002 Phase 2 data, the deal structure will set the floor for SPY002's BD negotiation.

BD & Deal Watch

Regeneron–Parabilis: $2.3B for Intracellular Access, and What It Says About Regeneron's IBD Posture

Regeneron's collaboration with Parabilis on Antibody-Helicon Conjugates (AHCs) — worth up to $2.3B — is a platform-level modality bet, not an indication play. The AHC technology fuses Regeneron's antibody engineering with Parabilis's stabilized helical peptides to target intracellular protein-protein interactions that neither traditional antibodies nor small molecules can efficiently engage. The deal logic is clear: Regeneron is building a modality portfolio that extends its competitive reach beyond the extracellular targets where antibodies traditionally operate. This follows the Q2 2025 GLP-1/GIP in-licensing and confirms a pattern — Regeneron uses external BD to buy platform capabilities at multi-billion dollar scale when internal discovery would take too long.

The more instructive signal is what Regeneron is not buying. Despite having no disclosed IBD pipeline beyond exploratory-stage programs and despite operating the largest antibody franchise in immunology (dupilumab), Regeneron has not placed a bet in the TL1A or IL-23p19 space. The Parabilis deal addresses intracellular targets — by definition, it is not a play on the extracellular cytokine biology that defines IBD therapeutics. Regeneron's BD pattern suggests the company requires two conditions before entering an indication via acquisition: (1) platform-level novelty, not just a better version of existing modalities, and (2) Phase 2b or later proof-of-concept. For SPY002, this means Regeneron engagement is plausible but only if Ailux can present the bispecific as proof-of-concept for a generalizable AI-native multi-target design architecture — not merely as an IBD molecule.

BD LENS

The $2.3B Parabilis total value, structured as a platform collaboration rather than an asset acquisition, establishes Regeneron's willingness to pay at this scale for modality differentiation. Ailux should frame any Regeneron engagement around the AI-native bispecific design platform — the ability to computationally design bispecific antibodies against novel target pairs — rather than leading with SPY002 as a single IBD asset. The platform narrative is the entry point; Phase 2b data is the credibility threshold. Regeneron is unlikely to engage on IBD positioning alone absent proof that the design approach generalizes beyond TL1A×IL-23p19.

Immunovant Batoclimab Wind-Down: $39M to Exit a Dosing-Design Mistake

The $39M payment to HanAll Biopharma to terminate batoclimab obligations is small in absolute terms but significant in what it reveals about the economics of program failure in FcRn inhibition. Immunovant ran two Phase 3 trials in thyroid eye disease to failure before reaching this decision — the sunk cost exceeds $39M by an order of magnitude when trial operations are included. The full pivot to IMVT-1402 consolidates Immunovant's pipeline into a single mechanism (FcRn inhibition) with a single molecule, eliminating the diversification that batoclimab nominally provided. Immunovant is now a one-asset FcRn company with a 2027 Graves' disease readout as its sole near-term catalyst.

For the broader landscape, batoclimab's exit removes one of several FcRn competitors from TED/Graves' disease and reinforces the trend toward mechanism-specific rather than broad-depletion therapeutic strategies. The FcRn class itself is not impaired — nipocalimab (J&J), rozanolixizumab (UCB), and efgartigimod (argenx) continue advancing across myasthenia gravis, CIDP, and other IgG-mediated diseases. The lesson is narrower: in diseases where IgG is pathogenic, FcRn inhibition works, but the therapeutic window is defined by dosing design as much as target selection.
